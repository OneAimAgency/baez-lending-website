This policy explains how The Baez Lending Team (Oscar Baez, NMLS #958051, with Capstone Mortgage Lending, NMLS #1973764) handles information collected through this website.

What we collect

  • Information you send us: your name, phone number, email address, preferred language, the topic you choose and your message, when you use the contact form or email us.
  • Calculator numbers: the mortgage calculator runs only in your browser. Nothing you type into it is sent to us unless you choose to include it in a message.
  • Language preference: we remember whether you chose English or Spanish in your own browser's storage. It never leaves your device.

This website does not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels.

How we use it

We use your information only to answer your inquiry, schedule appointments and discuss the mortgage options you asked about. We do not sell your personal information.

Mortgage applications

Loan applications are completed through Capstone Mortgage Lending's secure application system, not through this website's contact form. Information you provide in a loan application is protected under Capstone Mortgage Lending's privacy notice, as required by the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act. [Link to Capstone's privacy notice pending.]

Please do not send Social Security numbers, bank account numbers or other sensitive financial information through the contact form or by regular email.

Text messages and calls

If you agree to be contacted, we may call, text or email you about your inquiry. Consent is not a condition of any loan. You can reply STOP to any text message, or ask us at any time to stop contacting you.

Service providers

Messages sent through the contact form are delivered to our inbox by a form delivery service. [Provider name to be added once connected.]

If you choose to contact us or send your message through WhatsApp, it is sent and handled by WhatsApp under WhatsApp's own terms and privacy policy.
